# Byte Bridges India — Official Handbag Brand & Manufacturing Showcase

A modern, informative business website for **Byte Bridges India**, a premier women's handbag manufacturing brand based in India.

> **Note:** Strictly an informative corporate and fashion brand showcase website. It contains **no e-commerce, shopping cart, payment gateway, or consumer ordering systems**.

---

## 👜 Website Highlights & Structure

### 1. Luxury Minimalist Navigation
- Sticky header with transition from transparent on the hero section to an elegant glassmorphism cream bar on scroll.
- Direct links: **Home**, **About Us**, **Collections**, **Manufacturing**, **Quality**, **Contact**.
- Minimal mobile menu drawer with smooth toggle animations.

### 2. Full-Screen Campaign Hero Section
- **Headline:** *"Elegance Crafted for Everyday Life."*
- **Supporting Narrative:** *"Creating stylish, thoughtfully designed handbags that blend elegance, functionality and craftsmanship for the modern woman."*
- Slow image zoom (Ken Burns effect), dark/warm gradient overlays, animated badges, and dual call-to-actions (*Explore Our Collection*, *About Our Brand*).

### 3. About Byte Bridges India
- **Heading:** *"A Vision of Style, Design & Craftsmanship."*
- Editorial magazine composition combining brand narrative, artisanal benchwork highlights, and dual-layered photography.

### 4. Handbag Collection Gallery (8 Core Categories)
- **Heading:** *"Discover Our Collection."*
- **Subheading:** *"Designed to complement every occasion, every outfit and every moment."*
- Showcases 8 distinct silhouette lines:
  1. **Tote Bags**
  2. **Shoulder Bags**
  3. **Crossbody Bags**
  4. **Handbags**
  5. **Mini Bags**
  6. **Casual Bags**
  7. **Fashion Bags**
  8. **Everyday Bags**
- Each card includes high-definition lifestyle photography, tagline, description, and an *"Explore Collection"* action that smooth-scrolls and filters the showcase.

### 5. Featured Products & Interactive Silhouette Inspector
- **Heading:** *"Made to Make a Statement."*
- Showcases 8 sample handbag designs:
  - **The Aurora Tote**
  - **Bella Shoulder Bag**
  - **Grace Classic**
  - **Urban Crossbody**
  - **Luxe Mini**
  - **The Signature Satchel**
  - **Everyday Elegance**
  - **Modern Muse**
- **Interactive Product Modal:** Clicking any handbag opens an editorial detail modal displaying materials, hardware specifications, lining, dimensions, and craft highlights, with a 1-click *"Inquire About This Design"* button that auto-populates the inquiry form.

### 6. Manufacturing & Production Journey (4-Step Timeline)
- **Heading:** *"From Concept to Creation."*
- **Supporting Narrative:** *"Every handbag begins with an idea and takes shape through careful design, material selection, skilled workmanship and attention to finishing."*
- Interactive phase switcher:
  - **01 — Design & Inspiration**
  - **02 — Material Selection**
  - **03 — Production & Craftsmanship**
  - **04 — Finishing & Quality Inspection**
- Detailed benchmarks, workshop photography, and quality assurance standards.

### 7. Why Byte Bridges India (Core Principles)
- **Heading:** *"What Defines Our Approach."*
- 4 core value cards:
  - **Thoughtful Designs**
  - **Quality-Focused Manufacturing**
  - **Style & Functionality**
  - **Attention to Detail**

### 8. Visual Brand Story
- **Headline:** *"Style Is in Every Detail."*
- Full-width editorial fashion campaign section with typography and campaign photography.

### 9. Visual Archives & Masonry Gallery
- **Heading:** *"A Closer Look."*
- Showcases leather grain close-ups, hand-edge painting, precision saddle-stitching, brass hardware, and lifestyle photography.
- **Full-Screen Lightbox Modal:** Full image zoom, captions, and keyboard navigation (Left/Right arrow keys and Escape key).

### 10. Contact & Business Inquiries
- **Heading:** *"Let's Create Something Beautiful."*
- Professional inquiry form (Full Name, Business Email, Phone, Inquiry Nature, Subject, Detailed Requirements) with front-end validation and instant reference ID feedback.
- Contact cards with placeholder email, phone, physical manufacturing hub address in NCR India, working hours, and social media links.

### 11. Modern Footer & Legal Modals
- Brand overview, site index, social channels, and interactive **Privacy Policy** & **Terms & Conditions** disclosure modals.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework:** React 18 with TypeScript
- **Bundler & Dev Server:** Vite 6
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with custom luxury color palette (`luxury-cream`, `luxury-sand`, `luxury-espresso`, `luxury-gold`, `luxury-dark`)
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel, Playfair Display & Plus Jakarta Sans via Google Fonts
- **Animations:** Framer Motion & CSS transitions
- **Icons:** Lucide React
