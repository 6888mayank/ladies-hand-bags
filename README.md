A modern, luxury, informative business website for **Byte Bridges India**, a premier women's handbag manufacturing brand based in New Delhi, India.

> 🌐 **Live Website Link:** **[https://byte-bridges-india.vercel.app](https://byte-bridges-india.vercel.app)**
>
> **Note:** Strictly an informative corporate and fashion brand showcase website. It contains **no e-commerce, shopping cart, payment gateway, or consumer ordering systems**.

---

## 👜 Website Highlights & Structure

### 1. Luxury Minimalist Navigation
- Sticky header with transition from transparent on the hero section to an elegant glassmorphism cream bar on scroll.
- Official **Byte Bridges India** brand logo badge.
- Direct links: **Home**, **About Us**, **Collections**, **Manufacturing**, **Quality**, **Contact**.
- Minimal mobile menu drawer with smooth toggle animations.

### 2. Full-Screen Campaign Hero Section
- Official brand logo plaque centerpiece.
- **Headline:** *"Elegance Crafted for Everyday Life."*
- **Supporting Narrative:** *"Creating stylish, thoughtfully designed handbags that blend elegance, functionality and craftsmanship for the modern woman."*
- Slow image zoom (Ken Burns effect), dark/warm gradient overlays, animated badges, and dual call-to-actions (*Explore Our Collection*, *About Our Brand*).

### 3. About Byte Bridges India
- **Heading:** *"A Vision of Style, Design & Craftsmanship."*
- Editorial magazine composition combining brand narrative, studio product photography of the Midnight Black Quilted Handbag, and artisanal benchwork highlights.

### 4. Handbag Collection Gallery
- **Heading:** *"Discover Our Collection."*
- **Subheading:** *"Designed to complement every occasion, every outfit and every moment."*
- Showcases real manufactured silhouette lines:
  - **Tote Bags (Executive Cognac Tan & Jet Black)**
  - **Quilted Ladies Handbags (Dusty Rose & Midnight Black)**
  - **Fancy Dual-Carry Handbags (Handbag + Shoulder Bag)**
  - **Everyday Multi-Compartment Bags (Triple Internal Chambers)**
  - **Crossbody & Satchels**
  - **Executive Black Totes**

### 5. Featured Products & Interactive 360° Silhouette Inspector
- **Heading:** *"Made to Make a Statement."*
- Showcases real handbag models with real specifications and materials.
- **Interactive Product Modal:** Clicking any handbag opens an editorial detail modal with:
  - **Perspective & Color Variant Switcher:** Toggle between Primary View, 360° Internal & Rear Pocket View, and Black / Mauve / Tan color options!
  - Specifications (Materials, Gilded Rivets, Internal Dividers, Dimensions).
  - 1-click *"Inquire About This Design"* button that auto-populates the inquiry form.

### 6. Manufacturing & Production Journey (4-Step Timeline)
- **Heading:** *"From Concept to Creation."*
- **Supporting Narrative:** *"Every handbag begins with an idea and takes shape through careful design, material selection, skilled workmanship and attention to finishing."*
- Interactive phase switcher:
  - **01 — Design & Inspiration**
  - **02 — Material Selection**
  - **03 — Production & Craftsmanship**
  - **04 — Finishing & Quality Inspection**

### 7. Why Byte Bridges India (Core Principles)
- **Heading:** *"What Defines Our Approach."*
- 4 core value cards:
  - **Thoughtful Designs**
  - **Quality-Focused Manufacturing**
  - **Style & Functionality**
  - **Attention to Detail**

### 8. Visual Brand Story
- **Headline:** *"Style Is in Every Detail."*
- Full-width editorial fashion campaign atmosphere with typography and campaign photography.

### 9. Visual Archives & Masonry Gallery
- **Heading:** *"A Closer Look."*
- Real close-ups of diamond quilting, gold hardware, triple internal compartments, and finished handbags with full-screen lightbox modal.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Framework:** React 18 with TypeScript
- **Bundler & Dev Server:** Vite 6
- **Deployment:** Vercel Production
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS with custom luxury color palette (`luxury-cream`, `luxury-sand`, `luxury-espresso`, `luxury-gold`, `luxury-dark`)
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel, Playfair Display & Plus Jakarta Sans via Google Fonts
- **Animations:** Framer Motion & CSS transitions
- **Icons:** Lucide React
